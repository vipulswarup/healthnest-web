# SanoVault competitive analysis

Reviewed on 19 November 2026. This is a public-source review of nine personal health record and caregiving products. Sources are publisher guides, product pages, release notes, store listings, and National Health Authority material. Descriptions of workflows come from those sources. Website illustrations are identified where relevant. No authenticated workflows, device behavior, access controls, compliance, AI accuracy, or task times were tested. Missing evidence means the comparison leaves that capability open.

A family profile is a record space managed under an account. Delegation means another person can sign in and retain scoped access to that record space or selected caregiving functions. A share link gives a recipient access to selected material. The three experiences answer different needs, so they are evaluated separately.

The [SanoVault roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md) uses these findings to prioritize camera and file intake, the native share sheet, review before filing, and short-lived doctor handoff links. Direct WhatsApp forwarding is deferred. A user can still bring a file from WhatsApp through the operating system’s share sheet where supported, and send a doctor an outgoing handoff link through a messaging app.

## UX patterns by job

### Bring in existing records

- **MedFiles.ai is the benchmark for collecting scattered history and receiving future reports.** A user can either connect accounts and choose what to import or give providers a profile-specific inbox address. The inbox guide describes routing attachments into the appropriate profile after review. This creates a usable path for both the records already in a mailbox and the next lab result. Connector scope, duplicate handling, and the profile assignment review are the details to inspect hands-on. [Homepage](https://medfiles.ai/en) · [Patient guide](https://medfiles.ai/en/patient-guide) · [Family profiles](https://medfiles.ai/en/family-medical-records)
- **HealthKin is the benchmark for reviewing a single scan.** Its public sample progresses from capture and crop to extraction, flagged fields, user correction, and saved record. For a family vault, the review screen is where wrong-person assignments and incorrect report dates can be caught before the file changes anyone’s history. The sample search and capture timings are illustrative. [Product walkthrough](https://www.healthkin.io/)
- **Neela and DRiefcase show two other ways to add context.** Neela’s Scribe captures information spoken in a visit and makes a written artifact available to the care network. DRiefcase’s publisher materials describe email intake and assisted digitization of paper archives. The assisted scanning materials vary in age, so service availability would need confirmation before using it as a current market benchmark. [Neela Scribe instructions](https://www.neelacares.com/explore-neela/scribe) · [DRiefcase homepage](https://www.driefcase.com/) · [DRiefcase scanning information](https://paytm.driefcase.com/Home.aspx)

### Share the work of caring for someone

- **Neela has the clearest documented delegation workflow.** The care network assigns per-section access, allowing someone to help with schedules and tasks while access to other sections is configured independently. Its public guide and pricing describe named access modes and limited roles. The commercial packaging is also informative: the publisher currently lists a seven-day trial and monthly plans of $12 for a four-person care network and $24 for unlimited members. [Features guide](https://www.neelacares.com/explore-neela/features) · [Pricing](https://www.neelacares.com/pricing)
- **HealthKin documents the record-level alternative.** Share one record or a whole profile with a person or group, then revoke access. Its transfer at 16 is a useful lifecycle example for dependents whose records need to move under their own control. The public sources leave write permissions, ownership recovery, and adult-profile transfer details open. [Product and FAQ](https://www.healthkin.io/) · [Caregiver guide](https://www.healthkin.io/caregivers/)
- **The other products chiefly document managed profiles or recipient sharing.** Those tools can help a household organize records and send them to others. Separate caregiver sign-in, persistent view or edit scope, invitation acceptance, and revocation need their own checks. DRiefcase is particularly explicit about the master account and about distinct expiring record links. [MedFiles family profiles](https://medfiles.ai/en/family-medical-records) · [DRiefcase terms](https://www.driefcase.com/terms-and-conditions/) · [DRiefcase FAQ](https://paytm.driefcase.com/FAQ.aspx)

### Prepare for a visit or second opinion

- **OncoVault offers the strongest public example of a specialty timeline.** The oncology demo organizes reports around treatment milestones and puts key biomarkers and a short case summary alongside the timeline. Its value as a benchmark is the ability to connect a clinically meaningful event back to a report and make a longitudinal case reviewable. Automated stage or biomarker interpretation, wrong-patient detection, and the advertised summary time were untested. The publisher’s case-sharing example for clinicians and time-bound patient report links should be treated as distinct flows. [Product page](https://bigohealth.com/oncovault) · [Clinician workflow](https://bigohealth.com/oncovault/for-doctors) · [Report-sharing policy](https://bigohealth.com/privacy-policy)
- **MedFiles and DRiefcase show useful recurring retrieval patterns.** MedFiles describes attaching records to an appointment and retrieving with folders, type, and search. DRiefcase describes auto-indexing and an Easy Access area for records people expect to use repeatedly. Seht and MyDigiRecords add monitoring views for reminders and trends, while Swaral’s illustrated prescription view focuses on a single report’s extracted fields. Those are different entry points into the same underlying patient history. [MedFiles patient guide](https://medfiles.ai/en/patient-guide) · [DRiefcase FAQ](https://paytm.driefcase.com/FAQ.aspx) · [Seht](https://www.seht.in/) · [MyDigiRecords](https://mydigirecords.ai/) · [Swaral](https://www.swarall.com/)

### Share during an emergency or at a hospital

- **Emergency QR and hospital registration QR solve different problems.** The exact MediVault listing advertises no-login emergency sharing. MedFiles describes choosing what appears on its emergency ID, with a link, QR, or wallet pass. In the NHA example, a hospital QR supports consented profile sharing to receive a registration token. A QR alone gives little evidence of who can open what, for how long, or with which consent. [MediVault Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.base68bfe00bb9c47d75fccda3a6.app&hl=en_IN) · [MedFiles patient guide](https://medfiles.ai/en/patient-guide) · [MedFiles terms](https://medfiles.ai/en/terms) · [NHA Scan & Share guide](https://abdm.gov.in/strapicms/uploads/ABDM_STANDEE_24aabea939.pdf)
- **DRiefcase has the clearest public evidence in this set for the India registration and records workflow.** Its store listing documents ABHA creation and record linking, and NHA materials show the app in Scan & Share. MyDigiRecords is also a relevant India benchmark; its publisher advertises ABHA creation and FHIR R4/ABDM capabilities. This review did not test exchange, verify approval status, or infer live provider interoperability from email import, clinician links, or a file format. [DRiefcase Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en_IN&id=apps.driefcase.com) · [NHA Scan & Share guide](https://abdm.gov.in/strapicms/uploads/ABDM_STANDEE_24aabea939.pdf) · [MyDigiRecords India](https://mydigirecords.ai/in/) · [MyDigiRecords interoperability guide](https://mydigirecords.ai/abha-app-vs-mydigirecords-vs-digilocker-2026-guide/)

## Where SanoVault stands today

This baseline comes from the repository and describes implemented surfaces, with production rollout and device behavior still to be verified.

| Job | SanoVault baseline | Gap shown by competitors |
| --- | --- | --- |
| File and correct a report | Scan, photos, files and native share-sheet import. Mobile supports offline queueing, password-protected PDFs, and a Needs review count and filter. | HealthKin makes the uncertain fields visible as part of capture. SanoVault should make person, date, and type easy to verify, and give the family organizer one queue across people. |
| Let a relative help | Named profiles, shared households and invitations. Regular record access follows household membership. | Neela documents permissions by section and HealthKin documents profile or record-level access. SanoVault should support inviting someone to help with one named person. |
| Find records and prepare for a consultation | Reports are searchable and listed by document date. Users can generate a doctor packet with medicines, readings and notes. AI report summaries in the web API are written for a practitioner and require consent. | HealthKin illustrates search and per-person chronology. OncoVault shows what a specialty timeline and second-opinion summary can do with longitudinal records. Seht and Swaral show patient-facing extraction and interpretation patterns worth testing with source references. |
| Hand off to someone outside the household | Expiring individual document links and live, revocable Doctor Access links for selected people. A doctor packet can be exported as a PDF. | Bring scope and expiry together in a browser link for a selected packet or set of records. MediVault’s emergency QR is a separate use case and can follow validation of the recipient experience. |
| Retrieve records from providers in India | The patient model can store an ABHA number. Product documentation does not establish an active ABDM record-exchange integration. | DRiefcase shows the hospital workflow in public materials. Validate actual provider coverage and consent before committing to an integration. |

## What to validate in a product walkthrough

| Test scenario | Evidence to capture | Benchmark |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Add a blurred multipage report to an older parent’s profile | Profile and date visible before save, uncertainty and edit controls, preserved original, duplicate handling, correction after save | HealthKin, MedFiles.ai |
| Invite a sibling to help with a parent’s appointments | Own login, profile scope, read and edit rights by feature, invite acceptance, audit visibility, revocation | Neela, HealthKin |
| Find the latest result and bring prior related records to a visit | Search and timeline filters, event-to-original navigation, profile isolation, summary provenance, selected-document share | OncoVault, MedFiles.ai, DRiefcase |
| Open an emergency QR on a different phone | Anonymous recipient experience, exposed fields, expiry/revocation, access history, poor-connectivity behavior | MediVault, MedFiles.ai |
| Register at an ABDM-enabled clinic and link the resulting record | Scan flow, consent and identity selection, token, linking status, delayed or failed sync | DRiefcase, MyDigiRecords |
